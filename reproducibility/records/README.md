# Numerical research records: sources and reuse

These files contain model-development features, publisher-label comparisons, chemical-data summaries, and retrospective evaluation results generated for the **GC–EI–MS Annotation Tool**. The original publisher MSP files and their full mass-intensity peak arrays are **not redistributed here**.

## Dataset attribution

- **MS-DIAL GC–EI library:** Hiroshi Tsugawa, [original Zenodo source](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21910638). The [CompMS publisher licence](https://systemsomicslab.github.io/compms/license/main.html) states that its MSP library files are provided under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**. The present files are computational analyses derived in part from that source.
- **SWGDRUG EI library v3.14:** [official publisher library](https://swgdrug.org/ms.htm). The library is publicly downloadable, but the publisher page does not grant a specific open-data redistribution licence for the compiled multi-contributor library. Data reuse beyond ordinary permitted research analysis should respect any applicable contributor and publisher rights.
- **RECETOX Exposome HR-[EI+]-MS v2:** [original Zenodo source](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14285896). The publisher explicitly specifies [**CC BY-NC 4.0**](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Attributable, noncommercial uses may be permitted under those terms. Commercial reuse beyond an applicable exception requires additional authorization.

**Important:** The top-level **MIT licence applies to the project software code**. It is not intended to license any third-party spectral database, publisher-derived numerical content or database rights under MIT. Reuse of this directory's files may be subject to source-specific licences and terms.

These records are intended for reproducibility and academic examination. They do not represent independently authenticated sample identities, new measurements, or certified future screening-error rates.
